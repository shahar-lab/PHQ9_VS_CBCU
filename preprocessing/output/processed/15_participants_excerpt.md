# Participants

**Participants.** 240 adults recruited via Prolific began the study. Of these, 185 participants (108 women, 76 men, 1 preferred not to disclose; *M*<sub>age</sub> = 38.7 years, *SD* = 11.5, range = 19–66) met our inclusion criteria and constituted the final sample; the exclusion criteria are detailed in the Data Treatment section. Most participants resided in United Kingdom (67.6%), followed by Canada (10.3%) and United States (9.7%). Participants identified as White (64.3%), Black or African American (29.2%), or Asian (5.4%), among others. Educationally, 71.4% held at least a bachelor's degree, and 14.1% were currently students. Regarding employment, 58.9% worked full time, 24.3% part time, 8.6% were unemployed, and 4.3% were retired. In terms of marital status, 44.9% were married, 45.4% were single (never married), and 9.2% were divorced, separated, or widowed. As for handedness, 91.9% were right-handed, 6.5% left-handed, and 1.6% ambidextrous. Mean subjective social status, measured with the MacArthur ladder, was 5.49 (*SD* = 1.54). Clinically, 23.8% reported a prior diagnosis of depression; 13.5% reported currently taking prescribed medication for depression and 15.7% reported having done so in the past.
