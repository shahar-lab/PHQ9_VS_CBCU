# Data treatment

**Data treatment.** Of the 240 participants who began the study, 33 did not complete both sessions and were excluded. Among the remaining participants, we excluded 9 who left the study window more than 2 times, or for more than 30 seconds in total, during the PHQ-9 or the CBCU task in either session, and 13 who made more than 4 errors on the task quiz in either session. No trials had a missing response time or choice. No exclusion was based on response times, which were examined only descriptively. The final dataset comprised 38,850 CBCU trials from 185 participants (210 trials per participant on average).
