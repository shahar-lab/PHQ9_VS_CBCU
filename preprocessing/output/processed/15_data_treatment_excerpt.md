# Data treatment

*Data treatment. During data preprocessing we excluded 33 participants who did not complete both sessions. We then excluded participants who left the study window more than 2 times, or for more than 30 seconds in total, during PHQ-9 or CBCU on either session (9 participants excluded). From the remaining CBCU observations we omitted trials with a missing RT or choice (0 trials, 0% of remaining trials). No RT-based trial or participant exclusion was applied; RT was examined only descriptively. This resulted in 41,580 CBCU trials across 198 participants (mean 210 trials per participant).*
